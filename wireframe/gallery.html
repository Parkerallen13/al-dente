Al Dente: all screens

Grayscale wireframe. Phone frames are 390 × 844 and tablet frames are 768 × 1024, grouped in rows by flow. Open index.html for the clickable prototype.

A. Onboarding

Flow A: sign up through to the empty library.

B. Save from Instagram

Flow B: Instagram share, saving, saved, collection picker, library.

C. Add a recipe in the app

From the + button. A photo or voice memo goes through Saving, then Review when the app is unsure.

D. Recipe and cooking

The recipe detail scrolls, so it is shown twice. A second recipe shows the photo and voice memo source.

E. Cookbook

Flow C starts on 14 Recipe detail with Send to cookbook, then 18, 19, 20.

F. Cookbook device

The physical e-ink device. It has 4 buttons: Previous, Next, Select, Back/Menu.

G. Profile

H. Community

Posts from the communities you joined. Save a recipe from a post, or share one from your library.

I. Tablet (768 × 1024)

At 768px and wider the tab bar becomes a left sidebar.
