Copycat Chipotle Burrito Bowl

40 minMade 3 times
Source
Instagram post@weeknightbowls
View post
Servings
4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ lb chicken thighs
  • 1 lime
  • ¼ cup cilantro, chopped
  • 1 can black beans (15 oz)
  • 1 cup corn salsa

Steps

  1. 1

    Rinse 1 cup basmati rice. Simmer it covered with 1¼ cups water and 1 bay leaf.

  2. 2

    Blend 2 chipotle peppers, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin, and 2 tbsp olive oil into a paste.

  3. 3

    Coat 1½ lb chicken thighs in the paste. Let them sit while the rice cooks.

  4. 4

    Sear the chicken in a hot pan, about 5 min per side. Rest it, then slice.

  5. 5

    Fluff the rice. Stir in the juice of 1 lime and ¼ cup cilantro.

  6. 6

    Warm 1 can black beans. Build bowls with rice, beans, chicken, and 1 cup corn salsa.

Grandma's Tamales

3 hrMade every December
Source
[Card]
Photo of handwritten cardOriginal photo saved
Voice memo0:48

"Beat the lard until it looks like frosting. Don't rush that part."

Servings
24 tamales

Ingredients

  • 30 dried corn husks
  • 1 cup lard
  • 4 cups masa harina
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 3 cups warm chicken broth
  • 3 cups shredded pork in red chile

Steps

  1. 1

    Soak 30 corn husks in hot water until soft.

  2. 2

    Beat 1 cup lard until fluffy and white, like frosting.

  3. 3

    Mix in 4 cups masa harina, 2 tsp baking powder, and 1½ tsp salt. Add 3 cups warm broth a little at a time.

  4. 4

    Spread 2 tbsp masa on each husk, add 1 tbsp pork, fold, and stand them upright in the pot.

  5. 5

    Steam, covered, about 1 hr 15 min, until the masa pulls away from the husk.

Lemon Ricotta Pasta

20 minMade once
Source
Websitesmallkitchen.blog
Servings
2 servings

Ingredients

  • 8 oz rigatoni
  • 1 cup whole-milk ricotta
  • 1 lemon
  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Black pepper
  • A handful of basil

Steps

  1. 1

    Boil 8 oz rigatoni in well-salted water until just tender.

  2. 2

    Stir 1 cup ricotta, the zest of 1 lemon, and ½ cup parmesan in a large bowl.

  3. 3

    Scoop out ½ cup pasta water, then drain.

  4. 4

    Toss the pasta with the ricotta, 2 tbsp butter, and splashes of pasta water until glossy.

  5. 5

    Finish with the lemon juice, black pepper, and torn basil.

Dad's Weeknight Chili

1 hrMade 5 times
Source
Voice memo2:14

"Okay, first you brown the beef. Really brown it, don't be shy..."

Servings
6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 1 cup beer or broth

Steps

  1. 1

    Brown 2 lb ground beef in a big pot. Don't stir too often.

  2. 2

    Add 1 diced onion and 4 garlic cloves. Cook until soft.

  3. 3

    Stir in 3 tbsp chili powder and 1 tbsp cumin for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Add 1 can crushed tomatoes, 2 cans kidney beans, and 1 cup beer.

  5. 5

    Simmer, partly covered, stirring now and then.

Brown Butter Banana Bread

1 hr 10 minMade twice
Source
Websitebakewell.blog
Servings
1 loaf

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • ⅔ cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1¾ cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 350°F. Brown ½ cup butter until it smells nutty.

  2. 2

    Mash 3 bananas. Whisk in the butter, ⅔ cup brown sugar, 2 eggs, and 1 tsp vanilla.

  3. 3

    Fold in 1¾ cups flour, 1 tsp baking soda, and ½ tsp salt.

  4. 4

    Pour into a greased loaf pan.

  5. 5

    Bake until a toothpick comes out clean.
